## What this licence covers, and what it does not

It covers **this index**: the extraction, classification, vocabulary, hashes and links produced here.

It does **not** cover the underlying determinations. Those are published by the National Financial
Ombud Scheme and the FAIS Ombud, remain their documents, and are **linked rather than reproduced** —
this package contains no determination text. If you want the text, follow `citation_url` to the
office that published it, and their terms apply to it.

It also does not grant any right over **personal information** in the source documents. Complainant
names were never extracted into this dataset, and respondent names are published only where the party
is an entity. See `LIMITS.md`.
